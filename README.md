# 🎵 Vibe Verdict 
(Description - 🎵 AI-powered music personality analyzer that generates archetypes, insights, and hilarious roasts from your favorite songs.)

Turn your favorite songs into personality archetypes, emotional insights, and hilarious roasts.

- 🎵 Analyze your favorite songs
- 🧠 Personality archetype prediction
- 🔥 Dynamic roast generation
- 📊 Community statistics

- 💾 PostgreSQL database integration
